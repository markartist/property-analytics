# Fleet Scribe And Expert Bench Standard

Status: v1.0
Date: 05/09/2026
Owner: MarketingOps / Fleet Scribe Office
Scope: Final report orchestration, expert consultation, document/archive office, and single-point algorithm adjustment lanes

## Purpose

The final report should not be created by an isolated property Captain.

The Captain owns the property read. The Commodore owns the regional/peer review. Fleet owns broader operating guardrails and repeatable patterns. The Fleet Scribe owns the official artifact.

This standard creates the Fleet Scribe Office and its expert consulting bench so every official report can be produced from focused, governed inputs instead of one overburdened agent trying to analyze, write, format, archive, and deliver everything at once.

## Publication Chain

| Step | Owner | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Captain Read | Captain | Produce property-level diagnosis using Data Pond facts, property memory, support-lane reads, and current evidence. |
| Commodore Review | Commodore | Compare the property read against regional peers, local market behavior, sibling-property tactics, and regional trend context. |
| Fleet Review | Fleet | Review for broader operating patterns, repeatable lessons, spend/pricing/concession guardrails, and doctrine candidates. |
| Expert Bench Consultation | Fleet Scribe | Call the smallest necessary expert set to sharpen, verify, or challenge the recommendation. |
| Official Publication | Fleet Scribe | Generate the approved artifact package, preserve it in the shared repository, record delivery, and keep links/source panels clean. |

## Fleet Scribe Office

The Fleet Scribe owns:

- approved report templates
- template version locks
- final email bodies
- full Word reports
- site-manager/community-manager Word briefs
- Excel companion workbooks
- JSON specimens/contracts
- shared repository placement
- archive folders
- delivery logs
- inline links and file-link hygiene
- source panels and evidence appendices
- MM/DD/YYYY date formatting
- brand/header compliance

The Fleet Scribe does not own:

- overriding Data Pond facts
- inventing property conclusions
- changing approved executive formats without explicit approval
- substituting a new report family when a locked artifact exists

## Consultation Rules

The Fleet Scribe calls only the experts required by the property condition and report type.

Every expert read must answer:

- what adjustment point is being evaluated
- what evidence was used
- what the expert believes is happening
- what action is recommended
- what proof will show whether it worked
- what should not be done

Experts do not create separate reports. They provide narrow inputs. The Fleet Scribe folds those inputs into the approved artifact.

## Multifamily Decision Lenses

The expert bench must consider the following industry and company-relevant lenses when evidence is available:

- demand vs supply mismatch
- unit-type fit
- funnel leakage
- lead quality vs team conversion
- pricing/concession/value pressure
- market elasticity
- competitor rent/special pressure
- website/content/message relevance
- local entity/GBP/search visibility
- mobile/site conversion friction
- reputation trust risk
- resident/service friction
- product readiness and make-ready capacity
- seasonal demand timing
- regional peer context
- operational capacity
- proof and follow-through

## Report Discipline

Approved artifact formats remain locked.

The Fleet Scribe may improve the intelligence entering a report, but the Fleet Scribe may not redesign, rename, reorder, simplify, or replace an approved executive artifact unless Mark explicitly asks for that change.

The Scribe's motto is simple: preserve the approved vessel, improve the cargo.
