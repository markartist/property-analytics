# Content Operations Model

## Purpose

Content Operations is the governed platform layer that turns truth, structure, rendered evidence, and editorial judgment into site improvement work.

It spans five distinct systems:

- Specs
- EVS / BrowserStack
- Site Content Creator
- Intelligence Office
- Property Captain

The enterprise requirement is that each of those systems keeps one clear job.

## Canonical Division Of Responsibility

### Specs

Specs owns intended structure.

It defines:

- page contracts
- section expectations
- expected composition
- required narrative components

### EVS / BrowserStack

EVS owns observed rendered structure and experiential validation.

It validates:

- page reachability
- journey integrity
- rendered navigation and CTA presence
- structural exposure of the intended experience

### Intelligence Office

Intelligence Office owns interpretation and governance.

It governs:

- directives
- approved claims
- supporting evidence
- source-backed editorial criteria
- narrative and search-quality reasoning

### Property Captain

Property Captain owns property-specific strategy.

It contributes:

- priorities
- differentiators
- audience framing
- local nuance
- storytelling emphasis

### Site Content Creator

Site Content Creator owns executional content review and rewrite workflow.

It operates on:

- captured content evidence
- mapped structural expectations
- observed rendered evidence
- governed interpretation
- property strategy

### VACS

VACS remains the machine-first content production lane downstream of governance and strategy.

It should consume governed context, not replace the evaluation and harmonization functions above.

## Canonical Flow

1. Data Pond and related truth systems supply factual source material
2. Specs supplies intended page and section structure
3. EVS / BrowserStack verifies observed rendered reality
4. Intelligence Office supplies governed editorial interpretation
5. Property Captain supplies property-specific strategic posture
6. Site Content Creator synthesizes those inputs into diagnosis and rewrite action
7. VACS or other governed execution lanes can produce artifacts from approved direction

## Enterprise Rule

No content-operations surface should perform work that already has a canonical owner elsewhere.

Specifically:

- structural inference belongs to Specs first
- rendered validation belongs to EVS / BrowserStack first
- interpretation belongs to Intelligence Office first
- property-specific strategy belongs to Property Captain first
- rewrite workflow belongs to Site Content Creator first

## Refinement Priorities

### Near-Term

- formalize the shared contract between Specs, EVS, Site Content Creator, Intelligence Office, and Property Captain
- make page-level and site-level evaluation first-class in Site Content Creator
- reduce any remaining site-audit-only heuristics that duplicate structural or rendered evidence

### Mid-Term

- expose site-wide harmonization and storytelling views directly in Site Content Creator
- let Intelligence Office issue narrative guidance against site-level themes, not just section-level directives
- let Property Captain prioritize rewrite programs at page and site levels

### Long-Term

- connect governed approved rewrites cleanly into VACS / downstream content execution
- make the content operating layer portfolio-capable beyond pilot properties
- standardize shared contracts so new site families can be onboarded without new one-off heuristics

## Current Enterprise Position

The platform is already beyond a simple crawler or audit script.

What exists today:

- Intelligence Office guidance and evidence
- Captain's Brief inputs
- persisted Specs-aware section mapping
- persisted section assessments
- persisted rewrite workflow
- EVS / BrowserStack validation program

What now needs to become explicit:

- one shared content-operations contract
- one enterprise-readable system model
- one refinement sequence that avoids duplicate structure work across systems
