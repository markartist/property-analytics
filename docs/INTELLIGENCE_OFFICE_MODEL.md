# Intelligence Office Model

## Overview

The Intelligence Office is the governed editorial and search-quality layer inside The Data Pond.

Its purpose is to make the guidance behind content generation visible, editable, and attributable before it influences property briefs, site content, or downstream product surfaces.

## Relationship To The Data Pond

The Data Pond remains the system of record.

The Intelligence Office sits on top of that foundation and translates signals, directives, source documents, and operator instructions into governed content guidance.

In practice:

- The Data Pond holds the canonical property and portfolio signals.
- The Intelligence Office holds the interpretation layer:
  - rules
  - criteria
  - source documents
  - approved claims
  - property-specific guidance
  - operator instructions

## Why It Exists

The system should not behave like a black box.

Content teams and operators need to see:

- which directives are active
- which sources are informing those directives
- which pilot-property rules are in play
- what Alex or other operators have instructed the advocate to do

## Primary Responsibilities

The Intelligence Office is responsible for:

- maintaining visible search-quality and editorial directives
- preserving source artifacts that justify those directives
- mapping pilot properties to revised URLs and active guidance
- exposing approved points and open questions per property
- capturing operator instructions to the property advocate

## Current Pilot Scope

The first live scope is the five pilot properties:

- Champion's Green
- The District Universal
- The Harrison
- Ventana
- Calais Midtown

## Guardrail

The Intelligence Office should orchestrate guidance around PIB and related content systems, but it must not bypass or silently mutate locked PIB generation/rendering behavior.

PIB remains canonical where PIB is involved.
