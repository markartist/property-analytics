# Property Narrative Canon v1

Date: 2026-05-17
Owner: MarketingOps / Content Operations / VACS / Site Content Creator
Status: Strategy and source contract

## Purpose

The Property Narrative Canon is the governed core artifact for property content strategy.

It is not a blog, report, one-off prompt, SEO audit, site rewrite, or publishing package. It is the durable property narrative source from which those artifacts are derived.

The canon answers:

- what the property should be known for
- what factual proof supports that position
- which renter intents and search opportunities matter most
- how the live site, blog, GBP, social, email, schema, and future publishing outlets should reinforce the same narrative
- where current content is misaligned, thin, stale, duplicated, or unsupported by evidence

## Strategic Decision

VACS should evolve from a narrow AI content generator into the narrative synthesis layer for Content Operations.

The core artifact should be a governed `Property Narrative Canon`, with downstream artifacts derived from it:

- Site Content Creator section recommendations
- VACS long-form content drafts
- GBP posts
- social drafts
- email snippets
- FAQ/schema recommendations
- blog/editorial briefs
- shadow-page or local page plans when justified
- Captain/Navigator website and content action recommendations
- future channel publishing packages

This prevents future content from becoming a set of disconnected outputs that each invent their own angle.

## System Boundary

### Data Pond

Data Pond remains factual authority.

It owns:

- property identity
- website URL and source identifiers
- unit availability, pricing, concessions, and floorplan/product exposure
- GA4, GSC, GBP, PSI/CWV, GTMetrix, Google Ads, reputation, and operating evidence
- DataForSEO evidence
- future Ahrefs evidence once onboarded
- source freshness, lineage, and gaps

### Captain's Log And Brief

Captain's Log and Captain's Brief remain operating intelligence.

They own:

- what has happened
- what has been tried
- what changed
- which constraints matter right now
- which recommendations and actions have been approved
- what proof is required after action

The canon may consume Captain memory and briefs, but it must not overwrite source truth.

### VACS

VACS owns narrative synthesis and long-form editorial expression.

It should assemble the canon, choose content strategies, draft longer-form property content, adapt editorial assets by channel, and preserve provenance for what it used.

### Site Content Creator

Site Content Creator owns live-site expression and harmonization.

It should compare the live site to the canon, identify section-level gaps, propose copy changes, and maintain page/section alignment with Specs.

### Content Office

Content Office owns distribution workflows.

It should derive GBP, social, email, video, and future outlet packages from the canon and preserve approval/publication proof.

- DataForSEO Business profile facts

## Canon Structure

Each Property Narrative Canon v1 artifact should include these sections.

### 1. Identity And Scope

- property id
- property name
- city/state
- website URL
- source freshness timestamp
- included source lanes
- omitted or stale source lanes
- approval status

### 2. Current Operating Read

- leasing pressure
- product or floorplan pressure
- pricing/value posture
- current offer posture
- reputation/friction posture
- website/search/local posture
- proof gaps

### 3. Positioning Thesis

The concise answer to: what should this property be known for right now?

The thesis must be supported by source evidence. It must not rely on generic apartment language or unsupported luxury/lifestyle claims.

### 4. Audience And Intent Map

- primary renter intents
- secondary renter intents
- local discovery intents
- conversion-stage questions
- renter objections or frictions
- content required to answer those intents

### 5. Proof Point Ledger

Each public-facing claim must include:

- claim text
- source category
- source reference
- confidence
- allowed channels
- forbidden channels if the claim is too limited
- freshness note

### 6. Search, Entity, And AI Visibility Map

The canon should define:

- target topics
- topic clusters
- priority entities: property, neighborhood, city, amenities, nearby landmarks, floorplans, services, local needs
- priority queries and query intents
- DataForSEO/Ahrefs/GSC support
- AI/LLM visibility evidence where available
- entity gaps that should be reinforced across site and channels

### 7. Message Hierarchy

Define what message belongs where:

- homepage
- floor plans / availability
- amenities
- neighborhood / location
- specials / offer blocks
- FAQ/schema
- GBP posts
- blog/editorial
- social
- email
- future outlet packages

### 8. Harmonization Audit

Score the live ecosystem against the canon:

- site message consistency
- topic coverage
- evidence-backed claim usage
- local/entity reinforcement
- duplicate or conflicting language
- stale offers or stale proof points
- page hierarchy and internal-link support
- LLM-readable clarity
- channel consistency

The audit should preserve conflicts and missing evidence instead of smoothing them into a confident narrative.

### 9. Content Stream Plan

Define the recurring content stream:

- core story arc
- 30-day GBP and social directions
- blog/editorial themes
- FAQ and utility content
- seasonal/local opportunities
- proof refresh cadence
- content that should not be produced yet because evidence is weak

### 10. Derivative Artifact Queue

List approved or recommended derivatives:

- exact site sections to rewrite
- VACS long-form drafts to generate
- GBP posts to prepare
- social/email snippets to prepare
- schema/FAQ updates to propose
- EVS or Website Change Watch validation required
- publication/proof requirements

## Derivation Rules

All downstream artifacts must:

1. Declare the canon version and property id used.
2. Use Data Pond source facts without inventing unavailable details.
3. Preserve the difference between fact, memory, guidance, and recommendation.
4. Reuse the approved positioning thesis unless the canon is intentionally revised.
5. Reinforce the same entity and topic map across channels.
6. Keep channel-specific tone and format without changing the underlying facts.
7. Label unsupported or stale source lanes.
8. Preserve Fair Housing and brand constraints.
9. Capture approval, publication, and proof status.

## SEO, GEO, AEO, AIO, And LLM Readability

The canon should optimize for search engines and AI systems by making the property easy to understand as an entity.

That means:

- consistent property identity across site and channels
- clear local context
- stable facts repeated in natural language
- crawlable pages and structured content
- answer-ready FAQ and practical renter utility
- evidence-backed claims
- internal links that connect related property topics
- external/local proof where available
- no keyword stuffing
- no unsupported thin pages

The desired output is not just ranking content. It is a coherent public narrative trail that traditional search engines, local search surfaces, and LLM answer systems can parse.

## DataForSEO And Ahrefs Roles

DataForSEO is the active search/environment source.

It should feed:

- SERP position and competitor evidence
- OnPage structural evidence
- keyword opportunity and demand
- business profile context
- AI visibility and LLM mention evidence where available
- Navigator/Captain website and content diagnostics

Ahrefs is a future authority/content-gap source.

Once onboarded, it should feed:

- backlink and referring-domain authority evidence
- competitor content gap evidence
- topic and keyword opportunities not visible from first-party data alone
- content performance and authority-building recommendations

Ahrefs must be added as a governed Data Pond source route before its evidence is used as a canon input.

## SEMRush Boundary

SEMRush has been terminated for the active operating lane.

Existing SEMRush history can remain as legacy evidence when useful, but new strategy, freshness, and content decisions should route through DataForSEO today and Ahrefs after governed onboarding.

Do not revive SEMRush-dependent reporting or content logic for new VACS, Site Content Creator, Captain, or Content Office work.

## First Implementation Slice

The first build should be narrow:

1. Select one property.
2. Assemble a Property Narrative Canon v1 from existing Data Pond, DataForSEO, Captain, memory, and live-site evidence.
3. Generate a harmonization audit against current site content.
4. Produce one VACS long-form content brief or draft.
5. Produce one GBP/social/email derivative package from the same canon.
6. Record source gaps and proof requirements.

Do not build a broad publishing automation until the canon, audit, and derivative contract are proven on a single property.

## Non-Goals

- No alternate PIB renderer.
- No new one-off SEO report family.
- No private VACS truth store that bypasses Data Pond.
- No content generation that bypasses Captain memory, Intelligence Office guidance, or source evidence.
- No Ahrefs ingestion shortcuts before governed source onboarding.
- No automated external publishing without approval and proof logging.

## Success Criteria

The strategy is working when:

- one property has a reviewed canon that operators trust
- Site Content Creator can identify site-section mismatch against the canon
- VACS can generate long-form content from the canon without inventing facts
- Content Office can produce channel packages from the same canon
- DataForSEO and future Ahrefs evidence is visible as evidence, not hidden prompt seasoning
- future artifacts cite the canon version they derived from
- the public content ecosystem tells one coherent, evidence-backed story across site, local, social, email, and AI-readable surfaces
