# Marketing Operations Charter

Status: Draft v1
Date: 05/04/2026
Owner: MarketingOps / Property Analytics
Scope: Department mission, operating boundaries, outputs, and authority model for portfolio marketing operations

## Purpose

Marketing Operations exists to turn fragmented portfolio evidence into operating truth, clear directives, accountable follow-through, and durable institutional memory.

The department is not a report factory. It is the portfolio's command-and-coordination layer for marketing performance, property intelligence, source reliability, and cross-functional follow-through.

## Mission

Marketing Operations will:

- convert governed source data into property-level and portfolio-level operating truth
- identify risk, opportunity, and constraint early enough to matter
- translate evidence into decisions, actions, owners, dates, and proof
- maintain durable memory so the portfolio learns instead of repeating the same discovery work
- provide leadership with a decision-ready command view rather than disconnected dashboards and exports

## Department Promise

When Marketing Operations is working correctly:

- every active property has a current intelligence owner
- every important recommendation is evidence-backed
- every source has a known freshness and trust posture
- every open action has an owner and proof path
- every major property condition is understandable in plain English
- leadership can see what changed, why it matters, and what should happen next

## Department Boundary

Marketing Operations owns orchestration, intelligence, reporting, memory, and cross-source reconciliation.

Marketing Operations does not replace:

- property operations ownership
- revenue management authority
- paid media platform execution ownership
- web-development ownership
- customer-service execution ownership
- source-system truth where a governed source of record already exists

The department interprets and coordinates. It does not invent parallel truth systems.

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Source Governance

- maintain governed property identity across all property-scoped systems
- monitor freshness, routing, and quality of evidence sources
- surface missing or degraded inputs before they break downstream reads
- preserve source authority rules when sources disagree

### 2. Property Intelligence

- maintain Captain coverage for active properties
- produce current property reads, watchlists, and action registers
- reconcile web, search, funnel, inventory, reputation, and operational signals into one usable view
- keep property memory current over time

### 3. Portfolio Command

- identify cross-property patterns, cohort pressure, and repeated failure modes
- escalate severe risks and recurring blockers to leadership
- promote durable lessons from property reads into shared doctrine
- maintain command cadences for daily, weekly, and monthly decision surfaces

### 4. Execution Accountability

- convert recommendations into named actions with owners and dates
- track open work, stale work, and blocked work
- verify whether changes actually resolved the condition they targeted
- preserve action lineage so the organization learns what worked

### 5. Reporting And Communication

- produce clean, decision-ready outputs for Captain, Commodore, and Admiral audiences
- maintain standard read formats, evidence lineage, and terminology
- ensure externally consumed artifacts are consistent with governed source truth and approved display standards

## Functional Pillars

The department operates through six pillars.

| Pillar | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Intelligence | Captain reads, memory, property diagnosis, watchlists, directives |
| Source Operations | ingestion, identity governance, freshness, mirror health, evidence routing |
| Performance Operations | demand, funnel, inventory exposure, pricing/concession support, recovery math |
| Experience And Visibility | site quality, SEO, entity presence, EVS, BrowserStack, search intelligence |
| Reputation And Trust | Reputation.com, GBP reviews, response posture, listing completeness, sentiment patterns |
| Reporting And Command | briefs, command views, escalation logs, operating cadence, executive communication |

## Standard Outputs

Marketing Operations produces a fixed family of outputs.

| Output | Primary Audience | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Captain's Log | Captain and support lanes | durable property memory and directive history |
| Captain's Brief | Admiral, Commodore, First Officer, property operators | decision-ready current property read |
| Captain watchlist | Captain and support lanes | active risks, blockers, and source concerns |
| Action register | Human and system owners | accountable follow-through with proof path |
| Commodore read | portfolio/regional leadership | cross-property pattern and escalation synthesis |
| Admiral read | executive leadership | top-risk, top-opportunity, and decision requests |
| Source readiness view | MarketingOps operators | freshness, routing, and source-confidence posture |

## Authority Model

Marketing Operations has the authority to:

- define evidence standards for marketing and property-intelligence reads
- block low-confidence outputs when source integrity is insufficient
- escalate when source gaps prevent a confident recommendation
- establish role, cadence, and memory standards for Captain operations
- require owner/date/proof structure for operational recommendations in governed outputs

Marketing Operations does not have unilateral authority to:

- change pricing, concessions, or operating policy without the proper business owner
- overwrite source-of-record facts in governed systems
- publish misleading certainty when the source chain is incomplete

## Success Conditions

The department is healthy when:

- property identity is governed and reusable across systems
- critical sources are current and mirrored where required
- Captain coverage matches the active roster
- action closure rate is improving and stale actions are visible
- portfolio leadership can explain property issues without redoing analyst work
- doctrine and memory reduce repeated manual rediscovery

## Failure Modes To Avoid

Marketing Operations should explicitly avoid:

- dashboard sprawl without ownership
- duplicate truths and local one-off property maps
- impressive summaries without evidence lineage
- action lists without owners or due dates
- stale memory being repeated as current truth
- source conflicts being hidden in polished reports
- property activation without a true Captain or support-lane coverage

## Operating Principle

Marketing Operations should feel calm, exact, and useful.

The department exists to reduce noise, raise clarity, and make the portfolio easier to run.
