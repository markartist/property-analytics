Executive Summary

This matched-pairs analysis evaluates three operational Resi-hosted properties against comparable portfolio properties matched by size, metro, and traffic volume. The controlled comparison isolates the Resi platform effect from confounding variables.

🎯 Critical Finding: Resi Conversion Advantage

Resi properties convert 2-7x better than matched portfolio peers despite lower performance across demand, engagement, and technical metrics. This demonstrates superior conversion optimization and represents Resi's core competitive advantage.

  • Camber Ridge: 3x better conversion (0.24% vs 0.08%)
  • The Delta Pearland: 2x better conversion (0.16% vs 0.08%)
  • Cendana District West: 7x better conversion (0.54% vs 0.08%)
Performance Summary by Property:
  • Camber Ridge: Portfolio wins 4/5 categories — Resi shows conversion strength but needs improvement across demand (+58% impressions but -72% clicks), engagement (+12.5 pts deficit), performance (PSI 54 vs 62), and trust (+37% deficit)
  • The Delta Pearland: Resi wins 3/5 categories — Strong demand generation (+132% impressions, +185% clicks) and engagement (+4.1 pts) demonstrate market fit, with conversion advantage offsetting performance weakness (PSI 36 vs 63)
  • Cendana District West: Portfolio wins 4/5 categories — Despite 7x conversion advantage, Resi lags in demand (+28% impressions but -64% clicks), engagement (+5.4 pts deficit), and trust (+9% deficit)

Match Methodology

Matched-Pairs Design: Each Resi property matched with 2 portfolio properties using weighted similarity scoring to control for confounding variables:

  • Metro Match (40 pts): Same metro area (10 pts if different) — controls for market dynamics
  • Unit Similarity (30 pts): ±25% units = 30 pts, ±35% = 20 pts, ±50% = 10 pts — controls for property size
  • Traffic Similarity (20 pts): Based on 15-day session volume proximity — controls for audience size
  • GBP Proxy (10 pts): Presence of verified GBP data — controls for local presence

Exclusions: All Resi properties excluded from portfolio match pool. Monteverde (pre-opening) explicitly excluded. Only operational portfolio properties considered.

Comparison Period: 15-day window (Jan 12-24) with 87% data coverage across all sources (13/15 days). Collection outage Jan 25-27 does not affect analysis.

Synthesis & Strategic Implications

Core Finding: Resi Conversion Advantage

Resi properties convert 2-7x better than matched portfolio peers, demonstrating superior conversion optimization that represents the platform's primary competitive advantage. This advantage persists despite weaknesses across demand generation, engagement, technical performance, and trust metrics.

Strategic Implication: Resi's conversion UX, call-to-action design, and funnel optimization are significantly more effective than portfolio properties. This is the core value proposition to protect and amplify.

Property-Level Insights

The Delta Pearland represents the optimal Resi outcome with 3/5 category wins. Strong demand generation (+132% impressions, +185% clicks) and engagement (+4.1 pts) demonstrate successful market positioning, with 2x conversion advantage validating platform effectiveness. Critical performance gap (PSI 36 vs 63) is the primary optimization opportunity.

Camber Ridge and Cendana show the challenge pattern: exceptional conversion rates (3x and 7x respectively) cannot overcome deficits across demand, engagement, performance, and trust. Both properties convert traffic more effectively but struggle to generate and engage that traffic at portfolio peer levels.

Strategic Priorities

1. Protect Conversion Advantage — The 2-7x conversion rate superiority is Resi's differentiator. Any platform changes must preserve or enhance conversion effectiveness.

2. Demand Generation Gap — Two properties show significant impression advantages (+132% for Delta Pearland) but one shows deficit. Click performance varies widely (-72% to +185%). SEO and CTR optimization needed.

3. Performance Optimization — Delta Pearland's PSI 36 and 14.12s LCP represents critical technical debt. Camber Ridge's 10.35s LCP also problematic. Performance improvements could unlock additional conversion gains.

4. Engagement Quality — All three properties show engagement rate deficits (+4.1 to +12.5 pts). Content, UX, and session quality improvements needed.

The controlled comparison design isolates Resi platform effects from confounding variables. Portfolio matches were selected based on property size (±25% units), metro proximity, and traffic volume. This ensures performance differences reflect platform capabilities rather than market, size, or audience factors.

Data Sources & Methodology

  • Google Search Console (GSC): 15-day impressions and clicks (11/12 days with 3-day API lag)
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): 15-day sessions and engagement rates (13/15 days, 87% coverage)
  • PageSpeed Insights (PSI): Mobile performance scores and Core Web Vitals (11/13 days, 85% coverage)
  • Google Business Profile (GBP): Local engagement actions (13/15 days, 87% coverage)
  • Contact Intent Rate (CIR): Calculated from ga4_event_facts table using normalized event set: resi_price_quote, resi_application_start, resi_apt_tour_click, form_submit (excludes phone clicks and direction requests to ensure apples-to-apples comparison)

Data Caveats & Collection Notes

  • Collection Period: 15-day analysis window (Jan 12-24, 2026) chosen for complete data coverage. Collection outage Jan 25-27 does not affect analysis (87% coverage achieved).
  • CIR Normalization: Conversion events normalized to tour/apply/quote actions only. Phone clicks excluded as Resi tracks them separately while portfolio does not. This ensures valid platform comparison.
  • GSC Coverage: 11/12 days (92%) due to 3-day API lag. Expected behavior, not a data quality issue.
  • Match Pool: 87 portfolio properties available after excluding all 4 Resi properties (including pre-opening Monteverde).
  • Engagement Rate: Calculated as (engaged_sessions / total_sessions) × 100. GA4's native engagement_rate field is NULL in database.
  • GTMetrix: Not collected or used in this analysis. All performance metrics from PageSpeed Insights only.
