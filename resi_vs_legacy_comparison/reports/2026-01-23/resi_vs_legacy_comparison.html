Section 4 — Executive Conclusion

Under equivalent organic demand conditions (≥300 clicks), Legacy sites demonstrate stronger conversion efficiency than Resi sites, reflected in higher median SERP CTR (10.93% vs 5.79%) and stronger engagement rates (61.8% vs 59.0%). The Resi experience is not underperforming due to visibility constraints—when demand exists, Legacy sites convert it more effectively. This comparison isolates site experience as the differentiating factor.
