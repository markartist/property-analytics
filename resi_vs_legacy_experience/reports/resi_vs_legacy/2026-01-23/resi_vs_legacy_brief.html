Executive Narrative

Across a 30-day window comparing high-traffic properties (N=2 Resi, N=11 Legacy passing all volume gates), the Resi site experience demonstrated lower SERP CTR, lower engagement rate, and mixed Core Web Vitals performance (LCP: 7.95s vs 5.48s, FID: 314ms vs 198ms). The extremely small sample sizes limit statistical inference—this is an exploratory baseline to validate the measurement framework. Results will strengthen as more properties migrate to Resi and accumulate traffic.

Guardrails & Interpretation

Methodology:
  • Medians used (not averages) for all cohort comparisons
  • Volume gates enforced: ≥300 GSC clicks, ≥1,500 GA4 sessions, CWV data available
  • Complete data filtering: Legacy cohort filtered to only properties passing all three gates (N=11 of 87)
  • Conversion proxies: SERP CTR, engagement rate, CWV (not lease conversions)
  • Internal comparison: Venterra portfolio only (no external competitors)
  • Sample size limitation: N=2 Resi, N=11 Legacy with complete data—insufficient for inference
  • Deferred: Paid Ads efficiency, meaningful actions per session (requires event mapping)
Interpretation: This is an exploratory baseline comparing high-traffic properties only. Results are directional and will strengthen as Resi properties accumulate traffic and more properties migrate.
